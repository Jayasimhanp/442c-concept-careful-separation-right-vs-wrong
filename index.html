Removing the Shipping Spacer (Steps 2–3)

Two photos from the manual show the sequence. Then compare the right way to the one mistake the manual warns about.

The sequence

Step 2. Disengage the rotary holder screws and separate the rotary holder halves.
Step 3. Remove the entire shipping spacer assembly from each holder half by pulling on the tab. Retain it for future use.

Photos: 442C XL Installation, Operation, and Rebuild Instructions, page 8.

Right way vs. wrong way

RIGHT WAY
Pull the tab. The spacer slides out along the ring, and nothing touches the rotary face.
The rotary face stays exactly where it was set at the factory, so the halves can go on the shaft without any repositioning.
WRONG WAY
Pressing on the rotary face while getting the spacer out.
The manual's caution: “Do not push on the rotary faces.” If it happens, re-install the shipping spacers to reposition the face, then start again.

Wrong-way image: the manual's step 3 photo with an added marker. The manual has no photo of this mistake.
